    pagesmith v0.2.1

    Shape your PDFs. PageSmith is a free PDF toolkit that runs entirely on this device. Files are read by your browser and never uploaded; there are no accounts, trackers, or watermarks.

    Text, signatures and highlights are added on top of a page. Editing the PDF's existing text isn't supported yet.
